## Controls / How to play

You are a getaway driver for hire in Solmar, a coastal city at golden hour, taking jobs from the fixer Marisol Vega. Drive to the marker, do the job, get paid — and lose the police before they take you in.

- **W A S D** — drive / walk · **Space** — handbrake · **Shift** — sprint on foot
- **E** — enter / exit a car (steal a traffic car if the cops are not watching)
- **P** — phone, Marisol's job list (six chained missions, ending with the last boat at the marina)
- **R** — radio on/off · **Q** — next station · **M** — mute · **Esc** — pause
- Mouse drag orbits the camera; the wheel zooms.

Wanted level runs 0–5 stars: patrol cars cruise, pursuits ram at 1+, roadblocks appear at 3+, a helicopter joins at 4–5. Break line of sight and distance to cool down, or pay it off at the safehouse. Park rare cars in the safehouse garage to keep them, and spend earnings on engine, armor and brakes.

## Play

Open `game/index.html` in desktop Chrome (or `node serve.ts` and visit http://localhost:8080). No build step, no network at runtime — three.js r170 is vendored under `game/vendor/`.
